# Search demand trends

Use this when analyzing external search demand with Google Trends.

## Core inputs Google documents

- trend over time
- top and rising topics or queries
- regional interest
- related topics and related queries
- topic comparisons across locations, categories, and properties
- industry-wide demand patterns

## Useful readings

- **Rising demand**: interest is increasing and may justify deeper review
- **Seasonality**: interest predictably changes over time and can inform timing
- **Regional variation**: interest differs by market and may change what or when to publish
- **Industry context**: broader demand is moving, which can explain site-level changes or reveal opportunities
- **Language expansion signals**: related topics and queries in other languages may support localization review when relevant

## Reporting guidance

- Trends informs strategy; it does not prove a topic deserves content by itself.
- Prefer topics that fit the business, audience, expertise, and current site.
- Distinguish query strings from broader topics when interpreting Trends output.
