# Business presence

Use this when auditing whether an official business is clearly established across Google's own Search surfaces.

## Core checks

- verified website ownership in Search Console
- claimed Business Profile for local businesses
- accurate address, contact information, business type, and photos where relevant
- knowledge-panel information and whether the business can request updates as an official representative
- organization details supplied through relevant structured data
- clearly surfaced customer-support methods

## Reporting guidance

- Keep this separate from local ranking advice; the question here is whether the business has established an official, accurate presence.
- Pair with `audit-structured-data` when markup quality itself needs review.
