# Search opportunities

Use this when identifying grounded next-step opportunities after the site's main blockers are understood.

## Core inputs Google documents

- Search Console query and page evidence
- query-performance patterns from Search Console metrics such as CTR, average position, and clicks
- Google Trends top queries, rising queries, related topics, regional interest, seasonality, and changes in interest
- known user needs and the site's current content coverage

## Opportunity types

- important queries already receiving impressions but underserved by current pages
- emerging or rising topics relevant to the site's audience
- seasonal opportunities that should be prepared before demand peaks
- regional or language-specific opportunities when the audience differs by market
- content gaps between what users seek and what the site currently explains well
- demand shifts that suggest refreshing or expanding existing useful content

## Reporting guidance

- Prefer opportunities backed by observed demand over speculative topic lists.
- Distinguish existing-demand opportunities from emerging-demand opportunities.
- Keep opportunity discovery tied to useful, non-commodity content rather than page proliferation.
