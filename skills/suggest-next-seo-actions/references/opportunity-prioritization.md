# Opportunity prioritization

Use this when turning audit evidence into recommendations about what to do next.

## Core rule

Prioritize the next action that removes the biggest current constraint or creates the clearest user value with credible evidence.

## Recommendation ladder

1. **Remove blockers first**
   - crawling, indexing, rendering, or severe trust defects
2. **Improve interpretation**
   - architecture, titles, headings, internal linking, business data, or media where the page already deserves visibility
3. **Improve deservingness**
   - make existing important pages more useful, distinctive, and satisfying
4. **Expand surfaces**
   - add differentiated content, media, local/ecommerce detail, or agent-friendly experiences only when the foundation is sound
5. **Instrument the system**
   - add measurement when the next decision would otherwise be guesswork

## Scoring questions

For each candidate action, ask:

- What current constraint does this remove?
- Which user or business outcome does it serve?
- What evidence supports the recommendation?
- Is it foundational, compounding, or merely additive?
- What must be true before this action becomes worth doing?

## Guardrails

- Do not recommend scale before quality.
- Do not recommend query-variant page production as a substitute for useful content.
- Do not treat "AEO" or "GEO" as a separate hack layer; use them only as lenses for how strong content, media, and machine-readable experiences may surface in AI-driven search.
- Distinguish "fix now", "test next", and "later if conditions hold".
