# Search essentials

Use this as the library's baseline doctrine.

## Core frame

A site audit should separate three questions:

1. **Eligibility**: can Google access a working, indexable page?
2. **Understanding**: can systems interpret the page, its links, media, and business context?
3. **Deservingness**: is the content useful, reliable, distinctive, and satisfying for people?

## High-value checks

- Confirm Googlebot is not blocked from important pages.
- Confirm important pages return successful responses and expose indexable content.
- Confirm links are crawlable and the site's important pages are discoverable.
- Check that page titles, headings, alt text, link text, images, video, JavaScript, and structured data support understanding rather than contradicting the visible page.
- Separate technical eligibility from ranking expectations; passing checks does not guarantee indexing or visibility.

## Audit posture

- Prefer people-first judgments over search-engine theatre.
- Treat spam-policy risk as a first-class finding when a tactic exists mainly to manipulate rankings.
- Avoid inventing requirements Google does not claim, especially around generative search.
